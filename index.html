⛺ WildCamp Ireland

A community map of wild camping spots across the island of Ireland, gathered from Reddit threads, blogs and hikers' reports. Facility walking times are estimated from OpenStreetMap data (straight-line distance ×1.3 at 5 km/h) — treat them as a guide, not gospel.

The unwritten rules ☘️

  • Wild camping in Ireland mostly happens on private or state land without formal right — be discreet: pitch late, leave early.
  • Ask the landowner when there's a house in sight. A friendly word works wonders.
  • Leave no trace. Take every scrap home. Bury nothing.
  • Skip the campfire — use a stove. Ireland's uplands burn shockingly easily.
  • No camping where signs say otherwise (e.g. the Glendalough valley), and never in a farmer's field with livestock.
  • Check tides for island and strand spots, and the forecast for hill camps.

Leave only footprints. 🥾
